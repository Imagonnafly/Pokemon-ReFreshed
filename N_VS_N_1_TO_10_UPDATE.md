# N-vs-N 1–10 update

- Team Builder cap: 9 Pokémon.
- Battle size selector: 1v1 through 3v3.
- N means the number of Pokémon active on the field per side.
- 1v1 continues to use the existing Battle engine.
- 2v2–3v3 use the new MultiBattle layer and multi-action UI.
- Multiplayer host/guest supports multi-action broadcasts and snapshots.
- Local opponent roster expanded to 10 legal fully-evolved Pokémon.
