# Fully Evolved Starter Custom-Type Refit

The 27 final starter Pokémon were refitted to the project's 14-type system:

- **Venusaur**: Nature / Dark
- **Charizard**: Fire / Air
- **Blastoise**: Water
- **Meganium**: Nature
- **Typhlosion**: Fire
- **Feraligatr**: Water
- **Sceptile**: Nature
- **Blaziken**: Fire / Earth
- **Swampert**: Water / Earth
- **Torterra**: Nature / Earth
- **Infernape**: Fire / Earth
- **Empoleon**: Water / Metal
- **Serperior**: Nature
- **Emboar**: Fire / Earth
- **Samurott**: Water
- **Chesnaught**: Nature / Earth
- **Delphox**: Fire / Mind
- **Greninja**: Water / Dark
- **Decidueye**: Nature / Spirit
- **Incineroar**: Fire / Dark
- **Primarina**: Water / Light
- **Rillaboom**: Nature
- **Cinderace**: Fire
- **Inteleon**: Water
- **Meowscarada**: Nature / Dark
- **Skeledirge**: Fire / Spirit
- **Quaquaval**: Water / Earth

Move definitions were also scanned and any remaining legacy type labels were converted using the project's existing custom mapping. The type chart/status/field system itself was left untouched.
